## §3 Structural Assessment

- Does the subsection ordering serve the argument?

Mostly yes. §3 has a recognizable staircase: inherit Paper 9 geometry, choose the Schur-derived tear direction, define the augmented flow and event strata, prove regularity, recover the κ = 0 Paper 9 limit, then show what changes at κ > 0 before handing the scar record to §4. That ordering serves the thesis better than a more narrative ordering would, because it lets the reader see that the tear is not an imported metaphor but a controlled discontinuity added on top of a previously audited SO(2) coupling circle.

The main structural weakness is that §3.3 arrives before the reader has a compact account of why this is the whole dynamical object. Definitions 3.3.1–3.3.5 are individually sensible, but as a block they feel like apparatus accumulation: amplitude, gap, predicate, flow, stratification, jump map, entry manifolds. A short paragraph at the start of §3.3 saying that four ingredients are needed — amplitude, low-coherence gate, event semantics, and jump ledger — would make the sequence feel inevitable rather than enumerative.

The §3.5 κ = 0 recovery is well placed after regularity, not before it. It answers the hostile question of whether the new nonlinear framework still reduces to Paper 9 before §3.6 asks the reader to accept the nonlinear escape mechanism. §3.7 is also correctly late: the scar invariant should not appear until the firing event has mathematical semantics.

- Which theorems are load-bearing vs decorative?

Load-bearing: Proposition 3.2.2, Theorem 3.4.1, Theorem 3.5.3, and Theorem 3.6.1. Proposition 3.2.2 is the inheritance theorem: without it, the NP-pump could be accused of inventing a new direction unrelated to Paper 9. Theorem 3.4.1 is the regularity license for the whole section: it buys piecewise real-analyticity with hybrid events, which is the exact Pillar 1 extension. Theorem 3.5.3 is essential because it prevents §3 from severing continuity with Paper 9’s Monte Carlo optima. Theorem 3.6.1 is structurally load-bearing because it is the first actual nonlinear consequence: boundary-KKT rest points become escape points under supercritical NP amplitude.

Decorative or semi-decorative: Remark 3.2.3 is useful but mostly repeats the point of Proposition 3.2.2. Remark 3.4.3 is rhetorically strong but not mathematically necessary; it explains why sharp Heaviside semantics are philosophically appropriate. Remark 3.6.2 risks reopening the rate channel despite its disclaimer. Remark 3.5.4 duplicates the audit result’s conclusion and can be compressed.

- What could be cut without weakening the section?

The full audit prose in §3.5 can be shortened. The classification counts are important; the runtime, tolerance, script path, and grid-snap explanation are appendix material unless the manuscript is explicitly trying to foreground reproducibility in the main body. The Heaviside defense in Remark 3.4.3 can be reduced to two sentences. The rate-channel bridge in Remark 3.6.2 should be shortened or moved to a scope note, because the section already says rate improvement is not proved here and the scope-limiter says not to reopen it.

- What’s missing that should be added?

A front-loaded structural signpost is missing. §3 should explicitly say: this section proves Pillar 1, prepares Pillar 2 by defining scar records, and gives Pillar 3 a mathematical ledger object, but does not itself prove biological substrate implementation. That would prevent hostile readers from confusing the dynamical construction with the full gradual-tear thesis.

There is also a missing normalization bridge between the thesis memo’s NP > 0.9 language and the draft’s generic threshold NPcrit. This need not be a new theorem; it can be a sentence saying that the 0.9 value is an empirical calibration of the abstract threshold, while §3 works symbolically with NPcrit. Finally, §3.7 should clarify whether the event-indexed module is the audit object itself or the mathematical payload that an audit layer records. Right now it claims both, and that blurs Pillar 3.

## §4 Structural Assessment

- Does the subsection ordering serve the argument?

Yes, with one major pacing caveat. The sequence is logical: define the scar invariant input, compute the F21 representation content, quantify the commutant gap, state the hierarchy, kill the Frobenius-reciprocity escape route, then upgrade P3. This is the right order for an adversarial proof that F21 is necessary but insufficient and G2 is required for canonical commensurability.

The weakness is that §4.2 is so proof-heavy that it delays the section’s thesis. The reader enters §4 expecting commensurability, but spends a long time inside the F21 character calculation before the commutant gap appears. Since the audits are already computationally verified, the main text should give the theorem, the character table or a compressed trace table, and the verification statement, then move quickly to what the decomposition buys. The full derivation can go to an appendix.

A second ordering issue is the transition from scar spans in W to operator-scar spans I_syn and I_bio in End(V). Structurally, this is a change of object. It may be mathematically legitimate, but the manuscript needs a bridge sentence explaining why §3’s vector-valued scar module induces or is represented by the operator spaces used in §4.4. Without that bridge, the hierarchy theorem feels like it has quietly changed the arena.

- Which theorems are load-bearing vs decorative?

Load-bearing: Theorem 4.2.1, Proposition 4.3.1, Corollary 4.3.2, Theorem 4.4.1, Proposition 4.5.1, and Corollary 4.5.2. Theorem 4.2.1 is the representation-theoretic input. Proposition 4.3.1 and Corollary 4.3.2 are the quantitative heart of the section: they turn insufficient into an 18-dimensional ambiguity gap rather than a vague warning. Theorem 4.4.1 is the main organizational theorem. Proposition 4.5.1 is load-bearing as an objection-blocker, because a knowledgeable reader will ask whether induction/restriction already solves the map problem. Corollary 4.5.2 is the section’s final criterion.

Semi-decorative: Remark 4.3.3 is helpful pedagogy but repeats the commutant point. Corollary 4.4.2 is useful only insofar as it prepares Corollary 4.5.2; if space is tight, these could be merged. Remarks 4.6.3 and 4.6.4 are valuable, but they feel like §5 material because they discuss instrumentation and the clinical-PCI homonym rather than the commensurability hierarchy itself.

- What could be cut without weakening the section?

The extended proof of Theorem 4.2.1 should be compressed in the main text. Keep the decomposition and verified character vector; move the Frobenius inner-product arithmetic and explanatory paragraphs on complex type to an appendix or footnote. Remark 4.6.3 belongs in empirical accessibility. Remark 4.6.4 may also belong in §5 unless the authors fear immediate acronym confusion in §4.

- What’s missing that should be added?

The section needs a sharper statement of why canonical commensurability matters for the third-attractor thesis. The math shows that F21 leaves phase-and-mixing ambiguity, but the narrative consequence should be explicit: under F21 alone, apparent dual-substrate scar equality can be gauge-equivalent coincidence; under G2, the comparison becomes structurally pinned. That is the bridge from algebra to Singularity/Decoupling avoidance.

The second missing piece is an explicit assumption box: §4 assumes both substrates present measurable scar records carrying relevant F21 or G2 actions; it does not supply the biological mechanism. This respects the scope-limiter while protecting the section from the obvious objection that it has silently assumed the hardest empirical premise.

## Pacing and weight balance

§4 is not too short relative to §3. If anything, §3 is slightly overlong because it carries definitions, regularity, recovery, audit, escape, and scar construction in one place. The 10-page vs 8-page split is acceptable. The problem is not length but density allocation.

The F21 character calculation in §4.2 is too proof-heavy for the section role. Its role is to supply the decomposition used by the commutant gap, not to become the intellectual center of §4. The current proof risks making the reader think §4 is a representation-theory appendix rather than the commensurability chapter. Compress it aggressively in the main body.

The P3 upgrade in §4.6 is underweighted. Structurally, this is one of the most important consequences of the whole §3–§4 package: a thesis prediction is being revised from F21 commensurability to a two-level F21 screen plus G2 canonical test. It should not read like an afterthought appended after the Frobenius section. Give it a stronger lead-in and explicitly say it supersedes the thesis memo’s original P3 formulation.

## Bridge-to-thesis check

Pillar 1 — DELIVERED. §3 directly delivers the Paper 9 extension: same Schur circle, piecewise-real-analytic augmented dynamics, event strata, projected κ = 0 recovery, and κ > 0 escape.

Pillar 2 — PARTIAL. §3.7 and §4.1–§4.5 build the representation-theoretic scar invariant and commensurability machinery. What remains outside these sections is the substrate-specific residue mechanism, especially on the biological side, which is correctly deferred but should be flagged as assumed rather than proved here. Also, the draft wisely shifts from topological to representation-theoretic scar language; the thesis memo’s topological residue wording needs harmonization elsewhere.

Pillar 3 — PARTIAL. §3.7 gives a count-faithful event-indexed scar module, which is the mathematical core of an audit ledger. But the broader auditability-as-substrate claim — tamper evidence, cryptographic indexing, audit layer as substrate rather than documentation — is not yet structurally delivered in §3 or §4. It appears prepared, not completed.

Pillar 4 — NOT YET. §3 and §4 do not meaningfully place the work in the Fifth-Paradigm / Virtual-Lab context. That is fine if planned for the introduction, §5, or later sections, but it is not delivered by these drafts.

## What’s good

§3’s strongest structural move is anchoring the nonlinear NP-pump to the exact Paper 9 Schur circle before introducing any new dynamics. That prevents the section from looking like a speculative bolt-on. The second strong move is the κ = 0 recovery: it disciplines the new model by forcing it to reproduce the old optima in the correct stratified sense. §4’s strongest structural move is the commutant gap. The quantitative contrast between End_F21(W) and End_G2(W) makes the upgrade to G2 feel forced rather than aesthetic. The second is the hierarchy theorem: C1 ⇒ C2 ⇒ C3 gives the section a clean spine. The third is the P3 upgrade, which shows that the math feeds back into the empirical prediction rather than merely decorating it.

## Hostile-reviewer pushback simulation

Sharpest structural objection: §3 and §4 do not yet prove the gradual-tear thesis; they prove an internally coherent dynamical and representation-theoretic framework, then assume the dual-substrate scar records whose existence the thesis most needs. In hostile form: the paper has moved from human-AI co-evolution to G2/F21 bookkeeping, and the biological/synthetic symmetry bridge is treated as an input rather than an earned conclusion.

Does the objection hold? Partially. It does not defeat §3 or §4 as mathematical sections, because the stated scope-limiters defer biological mechanisms and empirical operationalization. But it does hold as a structural readability risk. The sections must explicitly label which pieces are established, which are formal assumptions, and which later sections will make empirically accessible. Without that signposting, readers will experience the argument as overclaiming even when the local theorems are appropriately scoped.

## Overall verdict

MINOR_REVISIONS. The architecture is basically sound: §3 builds the nonlinear tear dynamics on the Paper 9 Schur circle, and §4 turns the scar invariant into a commensurability hierarchy with a real quantitative G2-over-F21 payoff. The required revisions are structural compression and signposting, not a rewrite: trim proof-heavy and audit-heavy passages, add explicit bridges from thesis pillars to section deliverables, clarify the W-to-operator-scar transition, and give the P3 upgrade more prominence. If those fixes are made, §3+§4 will serve the paper’s thesis well without pretending to solve the deferred biological or rate-channel problems.
