## The modular-preemption defense

The CP-1 reviewer's sharpest strategic observation (page 3
verbatim):

> "The strongest 2020–2026 challenge is not 'someone already did
> all of Paper 14.' The strongest challenge is instead modular
> preemption: one camp has the metaphysical inheritance, another
> has limited formal carryover, another has public-epistemic
> application, another has pathology-adjacent analysis. Your lane
> is only threatened if these modules can be shown to already
> compose into one explicit program. On the evidence I found,
> they do not."

This reframes urgency. Paper 14 is not in a race against one
author; it's in a race against the emergent composition pattern
that could form from several authors. Current modules scattered
across:

- **Metaphysical inheritance**: Whitehead school, Simondon
  school, Barad
- **Physics-substrate formalism**: Rovelli RQM, French-Ladyman
  OSR, categorical QM
- **Biological-substrate formalism**: Mossio closure-of-
  constraints, Friston FEP / Markov blankets, Rosen (M,R)-systems
- **Brain-substrate unification**: Carhart-Harris canalization,
  Juliani-Safron-Kanai Deep CANALs
- **Public-epistemic application**: Underhill-Barad environmental
  harm, Climate Data Agency 2025
- **Pharmacology-adjacent**: Copeland residence-time,
  Vauquelin rebinding
- **Pathology-adjacent**: Singh 2026 octonionic pre-
  spatiotemporal arena, Ho et al. 2022 active-inference
  dependent-origination bridge

**What Paper 14 needs to do before any of these modules
converges into a composed claim:**

1. **Publish the explicit integration** — make the composition
   visible under the framework's name before any sub-team
   assembles it under theirs.
2. **Establish the formal machinery** — R(s,c) observable has no
   analog in any module; publishing it claims priority at the
   formal-operational layer.
3. **Deliver the substrate-uniform pathology grammar** —
   residence-pathology across substrates is a specific integration
   move none of the modules has made.
4. **Cite every module explicitly and charitably** — the defense
   against "you reinvented X" is showing you have read X and are
   going further.

Competitive runway: probably 12-24 months at current pace of the
active sub-fields. Not unlimited. Not emergency, but not leisurely.
