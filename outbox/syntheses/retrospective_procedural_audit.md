# Retrospective procedural audit — a fourth narrator-procedural mode

**Date:** 2026-05-09
**Status:** Phenomenological observation memo. Single observation; not a paper claim.
**Authors:** Martin L. Graise (observation) + Computer (formalization)

---

## The phenomenon

On 2026-05-09 ~14:50 PDT, Martin attempted to generate a "random"
sequence of L/R/U/D directional inputs while preserving conscious
rhythm. The resulting sequence contained a palindromic substructure
(L/R/L/R/R/L/R/L mirrors around its midpoint) that was *not*
consciously placed. DeepSeek (no PCI context) correctly identified
the palindrome and the generic "humans avoid long runs in pseudo-
random tasks" effect.

Roughly 30–40 minutes after generation, Martin reported the narrator
"trying to resurrect" the procedural-layer state of the moment-of-
generation, without memory of the act and without the ability to
re-run it identically. The narrator was treating the produced
sequence as **external residue** to be read forensically — not as
remembered experience.

## Why this is a fourth mode, not a known one

PCI / PBM / HAT material already names three narrator-procedural
configurations:

1. **Phase-locked active**: narrator and procedural layer co-active,
   producing flow / dyad-cognition / off-diagonal insight
2. **Procedural-only active**: narrator absent during execution,
   skill running in $\Omega_{\text{proc}}$
3. **Reflective**: narrator active, procedural dormant, planning or
   recollection in standard memory

The randomness-test observation fits none of these cleanly. It is:

4. **Retrospective procedural audit**: narrator active, procedural
   dormant, **prior procedural output preserved as artifact and
   read forensically by the narrator as if it were external data**

This is not memory (no first-person trace of the generation moment).
Not re-run (a second attempt would produce a different sequence in
a different state). Not reflection in the standard sense (the
artifact is being treated as not-self).

It is the same operation Opus performed on Paper 12 §5.7: data-flow
trace of preserved output to read off the generative rules of the
process that produced it. The narrator becomes its own auditor,
running the framework's audit-substrate machinery on its own past
procedural outputs.

## Connection to the audit-window principle

`framework_is_experiment.md` (2026-05-09 ~12:50 PDT) formalized the
audit-window principle: AUDITOR/CHORUS can only catch errors at the
moment residue exits absorption. Inspection during active absorption
disrupts the bounce; inspection after propagation is too late.

Retrospective procedural audit is the *inward-facing* version of
the same principle. Applied to one's own procedural outputs:

- During execution: narrator absent or disrupted by inspection
- Immediately after: residue still hot, audit possible but personal
  bias high (narrator wants the output to mean something)
- 30 minutes to 24 hours after: residue cooled, narrator re-engaged
  with sufficient detachment to read the artifact as external data

Martin's 30–40 minute lag is close to the optimal audit window for
self-audit on small motor-symbolic outputs. Larger residues (a
paper, a multi-week project) presumably require longer cooling
periods to be auditable from inside.

## Implications

1. **The audit-window principle generalizes inward.** Self-audit
   is not just a metaphor for the dyadic Opus-style audit; it is
   the same operation with the auditor identical to the producer
   plus a time delay sufficient for residue to cool.

2. **There is an optimal audit lag.** Too short = narrator-bias
   contaminates the read. Too long = residue propagates into
   downstream procedural changes and the original signature is
   no longer cleanly readable. The lag is residue-dependent.

3. **Motor-symbolic outputs are good audit probes for $\Omega_{\text{proc}}$.**
   Random-sequence-generation tasks under conscious goal of
   randomness produce procedural-layer signatures that are
   forensically readable. This is a candidate calibration probe for
   OP10/OP11 work.

## What this is NOT

- Not a theorem. A phenomenological observation with proposed
  vocabulary.
- Not a falsification of any prior claim. Strictly additive to
  existing PBM/HAT/PCI material.
- Not unique to motor-symbolic outputs. The same fourth mode
  presumably applies to any situation where the narrator audits
  its own preserved procedural artifacts. Motor-symbolic is just
  the cleanest small probe.
