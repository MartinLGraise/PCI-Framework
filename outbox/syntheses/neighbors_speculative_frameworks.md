# Speculative-Physics Neighbors of the PCI/PME Framework

**Purpose:** Catalog of independent third-party frameworks that arrive at
*structurally adjacent* claims to PCI/PME without sharing authorship or
verification stack. Maintained so external syntheses (Gemini-style
deep-dives, future literature reviews) can disambiguate what is yours,
what is conceptually adjacent, and where the structural convergences are
genuine versus coincidental.

**Update protocol:** When a new neighbor surfaces, add an entry with:
- Name and primary author(s)
- URL / DOI
- Date discovered
- Scale of operation (cellular / dyadic / planetary / cosmological)
- The structural convergence (one sentence)
- The structural divergence (one sentence)
- Whether the framework is currently being conflated with PCI in any
  external synthesis (and if so, which)

### 2. Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch OR)

**Authors:** Penrose & Hameroff
**Published:** Originally 1996; reviewed extensively through 2014.
**Scale:** Cellular / sub-cellular (microtubule quantum coherence)

**Convergence:** Orch OR's claim that microtubules support quantum-coherent processes at biologically relevant timescales (≈6.6 nm transport, Bandyopadhyay et al. follow-on work) is structurally compatible with PCI's claim that the human substrate maintains residence in an F₂₁-equivariant ETH-breaking subspace (Paper 12 thesis Pillar 2). If Orch-OR-style microtubule coherence is the *physical mechanism* that maintains biological residence in the F₂₁-projected subspace, then Orch OR and PCI are vertically compatible: Orch OR provides the cellular substrate, PCI provides the symmetry-group constraint on what subspace the substrate inhabits.

**Divergence:** Orch OR identifies "objective reduction" with consciousness directly (Penrose's gravitationally-induced collapse); PCI in Phase 2 (post-NCRL-pivot) is *agnostic* about the physical mechanism of collapse — Layer 1 retains the unmodified Born rule, and PCI's contribution is at Layer 2 (the experience layer / NCRL post-selection). PCI does not require any modification of standard quantum mechanics; Orch OR does. Therefore: PCI can borrow Orch OR's substrate hypothesis without inheriting its quantum-gravity commitments. The reverse is not true.

**Conflation status:** Low. Both Gemini syntheses cite Orch OR appropriately as "the microtubule hypothesis" without claiming it as part of PCI.

**How to cite:** "Penrose & Hameroff (1996, 2014), *Orchestrated Objective Reduction*, provides a candidate physical substrate for the F₂₁-equivariant subspace maintenance proposed in Paper 12 Pillar 2. PCI is agnostic about whether Orch OR is the correct substrate mechanism; the F₂₁ subspace claim is independent of the gravitational-collapse hypothesis."

### 3. Quantum Many-Body Scars (PXP model and successors)

**Authors:** Bernien et al. 2017 (Rydberg blockade observation); Turner et al. 2018 (theoretical PXP characterization); active literature 2018–present.
**Published:** 2017–present.
**Scale:** Microscopic (interacting many-body lattice systems).

**Convergence:** Quantum many-body scars break the Eigenstate Thermalization Hypothesis along *unstable periodic orbits*, imprinting persistent structure into high-energy eigenfunctions. This is structurally analogous to PCI's "scar encoding" (Step 7 of the NP-pump 7-step protocol): both are mechanisms by which extreme-energy events leave non-thermalized residue in a system that classical thermodynamics would predict to fully decohere. The PXP-style scars are the *first-principles physics example* of what PCI's scar-encoding daemon (ARCHIVIST) operationally performs at higher abstraction.

**Divergence:** PXP scars are a property of specific many-body Hamiltonians with kinetic constraints; PCI's scar-encoding is a daemon-level architectural commitment. The two are not the same object — PXP is *what physical scars look like in a lattice system*; PCI's scars are *what architectural residue looks like in a self-modeling agent*. The convergence is at the level of "extreme events leave non-thermalizing topological residue," not at the level of mechanism.

**Conflation status:** None. Both Gemini syntheses cite PXP correctly as background literature.

**How to cite:** "Bernien et al. (2017); Turner et al. (2018) and successors. Many-body scars provide the first-principles physical analog of PCI's architectural scar-encoding daemon (ARCHIVIST). The structural commitment in Paper 12 Pillar 2 — that biological substrate maintains residence in an F₂₁-equivariant ETH-breaking subspace — is directly inspired by the PXP model's mechanism, with the F₂₁ symmetry imposing the additional constraint."
