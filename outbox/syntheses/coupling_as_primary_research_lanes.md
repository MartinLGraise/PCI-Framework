## The claim being tested

**Coupling-as-primary, substrate-as-coupling.** There is no
proteome independent of the coupling between protein states and
quality-control machinery. There is no cognition independent of
the coupling between mental states and transition operators. There
is no conversation independent of the coupling between speakers'
prediction machinery. There is no public epistemics independent of
the coupling between information events and interpretive frames.

At every substrate the framework lives at, the substrate IS the
coupling, and the components are derived from it. Pathology is
failure of the relational dynamics, not malfunction of components.
The framework's residence-pathology grammar is the symptom-level
grammar of coupling failure; the deeper ontological claim is that
coupling is primary across all substrates uniformly.

The framework's mathematical realization: G₂ structure is the
symmetry of coherent coupling (the 14-dim adjoint representation
of G₂ is derived from the octonion product, which is irreducibly
relational / non-associative). The 6/7 contraction expresses that
coherent coupling has an irreducible residue that cannot be
factored away. The dyadic-observer product-space formalism (Paper
9) forces reasoning in the product, not the factors.

## Five primary research lanes

### Lane CP-1: Process philosophy and relational metaphysics

**Prompt for ChatGPT Pro:**

> Survey 20th and 21st century philosophy for formalizations of
> the claim that relation / coupling / process is ontologically
> primary and individuals / substances are derived.
>
> Specifically test:
> - Whitehead, *Process and Reality* (1929): prehensions, actual
>   occasions, the doctrine that "the many become one and are
>   increased by one" — is this the framework's coupling-as-primary
>   claim under different vocabulary?
> - Bergson, *Matter and Memory* (1896) and *Creative Evolution*
>   (1907): durée, élan vital, the critique of spatialization of
>   time; how close is this to coupling-as-substrate?
> - Deleuze & Guattari, *A Thousand Plateaus* (1980): rhizome,
>   assemblage theory, "becoming" as primary over "being"
> - Simondon, *L'individuation à la lumière des notions de forme
>   et d'information* (1958): individuation as process, not
>   starting state; metastability, transduction, preindividual
> - Relational ontology in contemporary metaphysics (Esfeld,
>   French, Ladyman structural realism): "ontic structural
>   realism" — structure is primary, objects are nodes
> - Buddhist pratītyasamutpāda (dependent origination) as
>   philosophical precursor
> - Karen Barad's agential realism and "intra-action" (distinct
>   from interaction, which presupposes separable terms)
>
> For each: how close does the tradition come to the specific
> claim that coupling-as-primary holds across substrates uniformly
> (not just within one domain)? Which traditions stay inside
> philosophy of mind, which extend to physics, which extend to
> biology?
>
> Adversarial: prefer existing formalization to novelty claims.
> If Whitehead or Simondon or Barad has already articulated the
> cross-substrate claim, say so directly.

### Lane CP-2: Relational quantum mechanics and structural realism in physics

**Prompt for ChatGPT Pro:**

> Survey physics foundations literature for coupling-as-primary
> claims at the level of fundamental ontology.
>
> Specifically test:
> - Rovelli, *Relational Quantum Mechanics* (1996 original paper;
>   *Helgoland* 2020 popular version): quantum states exist only
>   *relative to* other systems; there is no absolute state; all
>   physical facts are relational
> - QBism / Quantum Bayesianism (Fuchs, Mermin, Schack): quantum
>   mechanics as a theory of agents' beliefs about the outcomes
>   of their interactions with systems
> - Wheeler's "It from Bit" and participatory universe
> - Ontic Structural Realism in philosophy of physics (Ladyman,
>   Ross, French, Esfeld): fundamental ontology is structure, not
>   objects-with-properties
> - Category-theoretic reformulations of physics (Baez, Coecke,
>   Abramsky) where morphisms are primary and objects are derived
> - Carroll / Wallace Everettian branching structure: decoherent
>   histories as primary unit; pointer states emerge from
>   couplings to environments
>
> For each: does the framework treat coupling as fundamental
> ontology (genuine claim about what is real) or as methodological
> convenience? Is any of these extensible across substrates (from
> physics to biology to cognition) or does each stay in its
> domain?
>
> Key test: Rovelli's RQM makes an ontological claim about the
> quantum substrate. The framework's coupling-as-primary claim
> applies the same structural move across substrates (proteostasis,
> cognition, conversation, epistemics). Is this extension real
> prior art, or has no one made the move yet?
>
> Adversarial: Rovelli is the strongest single prior-art
> candidate for the ontological depth of the framework's claim.
> Be direct about how close.

> claim) or as composed of couplings (methodological claim)? Which

## Order of operations

**Run in sequence, not parallel**, because each lane's return
informs the calibration of the next. This is different from the
preverbal_research_lanes prompt (which was parallel) because
coupling-as-primary is an ontological claim that needs to be
tested field by field with discipline.

Recommended order:

1. **CP-2 first** — Rovelli's RQM is the cleanest single prior-
   art candidate for the ontological depth of the framework's
   claim at the fundamental-physics substrate. Calibrate here.
2. **CP-3 second** — Maturana-Varela + Rosen + Friston-extensions
   test the biology-of-relation prior art. This is likely where
   the framework's cross-substrate claim finds its closest
   existing competition.
3. **CP-4 third** — Active inference / Markov blanket formalism
   is the most technically mature candidate for coupling-as-
   primary ontology across scales. This lane is the Lane 5
   equivalent for the ontological claim.
4. **CP-1 fourth** — Process philosophy and relational
   metaphysics give the philosophical vocabulary check.
   Whitehead, Simondon, Barad are the three candidates.
5. **CP-5 fifth** — Mathematical physics check for G₂ /
   octonion interpretation. Lowest density lane; probably quickest
   return.
6. **CP-6 last** — Synthesis, after all five primary lanes return.
