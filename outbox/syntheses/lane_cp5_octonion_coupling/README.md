## Three adversarial sub-conclusions

**Sub-conclusion 1:** The octonionic Standard Model program
(Cohl Furey, Nichol Furey, Ivan Todorov, Michel Dubois-Violette)
is overwhelmingly **methodological / algebraic, not ontological**.
The recurrent verbs in their primary papers are "realise,"
"identify," "deduce," "encode," "break," "interpret geometrically."
None argues that octonion non-associativity means reality is
fundamentally made of irreducible couplings or that G₂ should be
read as the symmetry of coupling structures across substrates.

**Sub-conclusion 2:** No G₂/octonion paper ties the structure to a
recognizable "coupling-failure" or "residence-pathology" observable.
The nearest miss is Marrani, Corradetti & Zucconi 2025 on Okubo
algebra — they speculate that unusual non-associative properties
"may be related" to asymptotic freedom and confinement, while
explicitly noting "the actual mechanisms remain to be
investigated." That is not G₂, not octonion non-associativity in
the framework's sense, and not a cross-substrate observable
program. Worth citing as a near miss.

**Sub-conclusion 3:** The closest recent ontological neighbor is
**Tejinder P. Singh's 2026 survey** of the octonionic unification
program. Singh explicitly speaks of a "single pre-spatiotemporal
algebraic arena" and says octonion automorphisms are the "seed
algebraic symmetry." This is unmistakably an ontological move in
mathematical physics. **But Singh still does NOT say that G₂ is
the symmetry of coherent coupling, and does NOT interpret
octonionic non-associativity as the reason coupling is irreducibly
relational.** Singh is the closest adversarial hedge to cite — but
the thesis is different.

## Critical recommendation: SEPARATE the mathematical-physics paper from Paper 14

Verbatim from page 4, the reviewer's load-bearing recommendation:

> "I would NOT make the full G₂-as-coupling-ontology case in Paper
> 14 §2 if that section is supposed to be the formal observable
> section. The surveyed literature gives you substantial prior art
> for G₂/octonions as symmetry, compactification, representation
> theory, Jordan structure, and particle-model organization, but
> it does not give you a standard accepted observable map from
> octonionic non-associativity to 'coupling failure,' nor does it
> give you a recognized physics literature on G₂ as the symmetry
> of coherent coupling. That makes the ontology claim look
> interpretive rather than load-bearing for the observable
> analysis. Reviewers in a formal-observable section are likely to
> attack it as overreach unless you already have a derivation
> from associators/G₂ invariants to observables."

**Reviewer's specific recommendation for handling this in Paper 14:**

> "In Paper 14 §2, include only a SHORT DEFENSIVE PARAGRAPH saying
> that existing literature already uses octonions and G₂ in
> symmetry, compactification, and particle-foundations contexts,
> but that you have not found prior publications making the
> stronger coupling-ontology interpretation explicit. Then DEFER
> the full interpretive claim to a separate mathematical-physics
> paper whose burden is to do three things cleanly:
>
> 1. Derive why non-associativity should be read as irreducible
>    relationality rather than merely algebraic failure of
>    reassociation;
> 2. Show why G₂, rather than some broader exceptional or
>    categorical structure, is the right symmetry notion for
>    coherent coupling; and
> 3. Define at least one concrete invariant or observable that
>    distinguishes coherent from failed coupling."

**This is the correct handling.** Paper 14 should NOT carry the
G₂-coupling-ontology claim as load-bearing. The claim is
genuinely novel, which means it needs its own paper with proper
derivation work, not a section in a paper whose primary contribution
is cross-substrate empirical observable.
