## PCI vs spontaneous LZc dissociation (strengthens Paper 14's three-level move)

From page 8 of report:

> "PCI (perturbational) and spontaneous LZc do not measure the same
> thing. PCI is a transition-evoked-causal-spread metric — it asks
> 'how rich is the response when forced to transition?' Spontaneous
> LZc asks 'how diverse is the trajectory at rest?' The user's
> intuition that 'PCI vs spontaneous LZc dissociation' maps onto
> residence-vs-state-identity is partially supported: PCI rises when
> capacity to transition is preserved; spontaneous LZc rises when
> the current trajectory is diverse. A locked-attractor state can
> have low PCI (poor transition response) and intermediate LZc
> (busy local dynamics)."

This is real prior art for Paper 14's three-level analytical move
(spontaneous complexity ≠ perturbational complexity ≠ residence
depth). **Strengthens** rather than threatens Paper 14's
contribution, provided Paper 14 cites Casali 2013 + Sarasso 2015
explicitly.

## Clinical four-disorder summary

Do depression / OCD / addiction / PTSD share a residence-pattern
signature, or distinct dwell-time pathologies?

**Distinct signatures, unified at meta-level:**

- **Depression:** increased mPFC-DMN dwell, reduced inter-network
  transition variability (Kaiser 2016, Wise 2017).
- **OCD:** increased CSTC-loop dwell, DMN-SN dysconnectivity
  (Collins 2024, Collins/Cipriani/Aaronson 2026). **2024–2026 OCD
  reviews do explicitly invoke compulsive-loop attractor disruption
  + canalization. The OCD residence framing is now mainstream.**
- **Addiction:** increased cue-reactivity / incentive-salience
  network dwell. Less directly measured as dwell-time; more often
  as cue-reactivity amplitude.
- **PTSD:** increased amygdala-salience network dwell, reduced
  amygdala-hippocampal contextual integration (Singleton MDMA
  2023). **PTSD literature predominantly uses fear-extinction +
  memory-reconsolidation framing, not residence-dwell-time.**

Unified at three theoretical levels:
1. Canalization (Carhart-Harris 2023): all four = deepened canals
2. REBUS (Carhart-Harris-Friston 2019): all four = overweighted priors
3. Deep CANALs (Juliani-Safron-Kanai 2024): Type-A inference vs
   Type-B synaptic landscape pathologies

## Subtle thesis correction

not produce new states." Canalization-as-stated leans toward TEMP
producing a transient new high-entropy state en route to landscape
recalibration. The distinction is real (Paper 14's stricter
restoration thesis IS a refinement) but it is a refinement, not a
paradigm shift. **Don't oversell.**

Better phrasing for Paper 14: "Psychedelics work by flattening
landscape geometry. Whether this occurs via transient state-
production (canalization TEMP interpretation) or via direct
transit-restoration (framework refinement) is a resolvable
empirical question; both mechanisms are residence-pathology
interventions at the τ_R level."
