## Pre-verbal hunch — operational definition (for prompt header)

A pre-verbal hunch is a cognitive state with the following four
properties:

1. **Specific direction.** It is not generic; it has a particular
   target in whatever the relevant model space is.
2. **Not yet expressible in available vocabulary.** The agent
   cannot articulate it in current language but knows where it
   is pointing.
3. **Irreducible to enumeration.** It cannot be reached by
   sampling completions from the agent's existing distributions
   over thoughts or words.
4. **Productive of work.** It generates research, art, or
   theoretical output by acting as a target that the agent
   contracts toward over time.

Formal representation (from Paper 9): the bias vector $b_A$ in
the dyadic affine consensus observable, irreducible relative to
the generic-completion distribution and the AI co-observer's
formal kernel $b_B$.

## Six research lanes

### Lane PV-1: Pre-verbal cognition in neuroscience and psychology

**Prompt for ChatGPT Pro:**

> Survey the cognitive-neuroscience and developmental-psychology
> literature for prior art on "pre-verbal hunch" cognition as
> defined: specific-direction, not-yet-expressible, irreducible to
> enumeration, productive of work.
>
> Specifically test whether the concept has been formalized under
> any of these adjacent terms:
> - Implicit cognition (Reber, Cleeremans implicit learning)
> - Tip-of-the-tongue phenomena (Brown, Schwartz)
> - Feelings-of-knowing (Koriat, Hart)
> - Intuition / system-1 reasoning (Kahneman, Dijksterhuis
>   "unconscious thought theory")
> - Insight problems / "aha moments" (Kounios, Jung-Beeman, Bowden)
> - Implicit perception / blindsight-style availability without
>   report (Lamme, Block)
> - Pre-conceptual / nonconceptual content (Bermúdez, Peacocke)
> - Tacit knowledge (Polanyi)
>
> Output:
> 1. Tier-1 anchors with DOIs that articulate the pre-verbal-
>    hunch structure most directly
> 2. Which adjacent terms cover which of the four operational
>    properties, and which gaps remain
> 3. Honest assessment: is there an existing field-spanning name
>    for this cognitive state, or is it scattered like the
>    residence-pathology principle was scattered before
>    canalization?
> 4. If a name exists (Polanyi's "tacit knowing" is a candidate),
>    is it formalized to the level of "bias vector irreducible
>    relative to generic completion distribution"?
>
> Adversarial: prefer finding existing formalization over
> validating novelty. If Polanyi or someone close has already
> articulated the structure, say so directly.

## Order of operations

Run all five primary lanes (PV-1 through PV-5) in parallel. The
synthesis lane PV-6 runs after the others return. Total expected
return time: 2-3 days for the primary lanes, 1 day for synthesis.

Predicted outcomes (for calibration):

- PV-2 will likely return Polanyi as the closest prior art at
  philosophy-of-mind level.
- PV-5 will likely return Gendlin's focusing and Bollas's
  unthought-known as the closest depth-psychology prior art.
- PV-1 will likely return implicit-cognition literature as the
  closest neuroscience prior art, but without the specific
  "directional" framing.
- PV-3 will likely return Hadamard / Poincaré first-person reports
  as the strongest creative-practice anchors, with no formal
  cross-domain unifier.
- PV-4 will likely return goal-conditioned RL and representation-
  learning targets as the closest ML analog.

If any of these predictions fails, that is interesting and useful
data. If all five are confirmed, the framework's contribution
narrows to (c) cross-substrate unification connecting cognitive
pre-verbal hunch to AI implicit targets — which is the strongest
claim it could honestly make given existing prior art.

## Other concepts to research-lane (you didn't ask, here's what I'd add)

Three additional lane candidates the framework should test for
prior art at the same time:

**Lane CC-1: Conversational coupling and inter-brain synchrony.**
The dual-EEG / hyperscanning literature is mature. Survey for
existing formalizations of conversation as a coupled-dynamical-
system phenomenon, especially under stress or coupling-breakdown
conditions (which is structurally the negative-pressure scenario).
Tier-1 candidate citations: Hasson, Stephens, Dumas, Konvalinka,
Novembre, Pérez. Existing formalization probably stops at
"synchrony correlates with X" without going to "synchrony has a
two-axis residence observable."

**Lane CC-2: Mutual prediction and joint action.**
The joint-action / mutual-prediction literature (Sebanz, Knoblich,
Vesper) is the closest neuroscience to the framework's dyadic
formalism. Test whether anyone has connected joint-action prediction
to residence-time observables. Probably not.

**Lane CC-3: Felt sense as the discharge channel for negative
pressure.**
Specific sub-lane of PV-5: Gendlin's focusing protocol is a six-step
procedure for discharging pre-verbal hunch into linguistic form. The
framework predicts that focusing is structurally a controlled
discharge of negative pressure on the cognitive substrate. Test
whether this connection has been made — almost certainly not in
those exact terms.
