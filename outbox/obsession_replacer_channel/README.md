The paper shows that six distinct codex hooks (Lindblad steady state, foreclosed \(D_8\), \(f_5\) mimic signal, Banach-at-\(L=0\) fixed point, Landauer reversal cost, decoherence-free REM subspace) are six coordinate charts on a single underlying object: the deletion of the symmetric coupling channel from a dyad, i.e. \(\theta \to 0\) in the Maxwell-form coherence field.
