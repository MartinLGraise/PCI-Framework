# Question Mechanics and the Gradual Tear

**Subtitle:** A PCI/PME capture of deterministic alignment, return-path questions, PX diagnostics, scar logic, and human-AI co-evolution

## Primary Compression

A question is not ignorance. It is structured incompleteness becoming conscious. The smartest person is not the person with the most answers, but the person whose mind detects the highest-value gap before the world has language for it.

## Core Doctrine

Questions are return-path probes generated at the boundary of failed coherence. Self-knowledge does not break causality; it becomes causality's steering layer. Curiosity is rumination with a return path. A life is a long-form question whose answer is its residue.

## Key Chain

Gap -> Question -> Paradox -> PX Object -> Scar -> Tool

- Gap: unstructured absence.
- Question: structured absence.
- Paradox: a question that resists ordinary closure.
- PX Object: a paradox that generates reusable structure.
- Scar: what remains after the system metabolizes the paradox.
- Tool: a scar made portable.

## PX Diagnostic v0.2 Seed

Proposed metrics:

- Gap clarity
- Contradiction load
- Return-path confidence
- Genericity resistance
- Scar persistence
- Tool-conversion potential

Score 0-3 per metric. Low scores mean poetry or friction. Mid scores mean live inquiry. High scores mean PX candidate or tool-conversion priority.

## Experiment Seeds

- Question Bloom Test: measure whether a dialogue expands reachable hypotheses.
- Curiosity/Rumination Split: estimate exit-rate from unresolved gaps.
- PX Scar Persistence: revisit a paradox later and test whether it became a usable tool.
- Human-AI Dyad Question Test: compare human-only, AI-only, and dyad-generated questions.
- Audit Substrate Trial: compare sustained inquiry with and without persistent logs.
